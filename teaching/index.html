Workshop in Learning Systems and Autonomous Agents

A year-long workshop for Computer Science undergraduates at the Academic College of Tel Aviv-Yaffo, first given in 2025-2026, on building AI agents: software that plans, learns from feedback and acts on its own. Students work hands-on with large language models (LLMs), libraries like LangChain and LangGraph, and protocols like MCP and ACP. Topics include prompting and reasoning methods such as ReAct and Tree-of-Thought, measuring how well agents work, agents that use outside data (RAG), multi-agent systems, and agent security. Students work in teams of 2-3 on a final project, either an app or a research study: each team gives a short hands-on session to the class, presents its project plan at the end of the first semester, meets me regularly to track progress, and presents the final result at the end of the year.
